User Manual & Guide

Learn how to use every feature of Etho Trading Journal.

Welcome to Etho Trading Journal! When you open the app for the first time, you will be greeted by a quick setup screen where you can enter your full name, preferred trading nickname, and starting balance.

The Dashboard provides a real-time summary of your trading performance. All metrics update automatically as you add trades.

Tap Add Trade to record a new position, select Long or Short, input prices, and save.

Review all past trades in a clean table or cards, search, filter, and delete as needed.

The Calendar view displays a monthly grid with daily P&L totals and color indicators.

Analyze your trading metrics and overall consistency.

Track discipline and habits. Complete or pause goals anytime.

Upload or capture a profile image. Your avatar synchronizes instantly across the sidebar, header, and settings.

Default starting balance is ₹0. Current Balance = Starting Balance + Total Net P&L.

Export/import JSON backups or clear trade history safely.
